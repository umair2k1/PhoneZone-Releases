# Phone Zone — Release Notes

## v1.3.5

Phone Zone continues to evolve from a mobile-shop POS into a complete **retail, inventory, repair and business-management platform**.

### Repair Management

- Expanded repair-service workflow with service catalog, repair intake and ticket lifecycle.
- Customer device intake with device details, IMEI/serial information and intake condition.
- Repair job status tracking from intake through delivery.
- Technician assignment and repair notes.
- Spare-parts consumption linked directly to inventory.
- Repair profitability and gross-margin tracking.
- Repair invoice generation with labor/service and parts line items.
- Customer repair history alongside sales history.

### Inventory & Device Tracking

- Category-aware inventory for New Phones, Used Phones, Accessories and Repair Services.
- IMEI/serial tracking for serialized devices and inventory.
- New-phone sales can be associated with the exact inventory IMEI for device-level traceability.
- Repair Services are treated as services rather than physical stock.
- Stock valuation at cost and retail value.
- Low-stock and out-of-stock monitoring.
- Inventory multi-select actions and safer item deletion.
- Expanded inventory pagination and export capabilities.

### Sales Audit & Reporting

- Inventory snapshot in Sales Audit reports:
  - Total stock volume
  - Stock worth at cost
  - Retail valuation
  - Low-stock item count
  - Out-of-stock item count
- Payment breakdown for Cash, UPI, Finance and other payment modes.
- Payment reconciliation and itemized profit reporting.
- Improved A4 sales-report layout and printing.

### Loans, Liabilities & Payables

- Edit existing loan/liability entries.
- Record repayments without overwriting the original loan history.
- Outstanding-balance tracking.
- Lender suggestions based on previously entered records.
- Improved due-date and liability status handling.

### Inventory Export & Printing

- Multi-select inventory export categories.
- Select any combination of:
  - New Phones
  - Used Phones
  - Accessories
  - Repair Services
- Select All / Clear All controls.
- Dedicated Repair Services export/print layout.
- Improved A4 margins, spacing and category separation.
- Excel export support for inventory and service information.

### Data Safety

- Transactional and versioned database migrations.
- Pre-migration backups.
- Schema validation and startup protection against failed migrations.
- Historical financial data is preserved during schema evolution.
- Repair-service and repair-job data included in backups.

## What Phone Zone can manage

**Sales** — POS billing, customers, payments, credit and invoices.

**Inventory** — New phones, used phones, accessories, suppliers, stock valuation and IMEI/serial tracking.

**Repairs** — Device intake, diagnosis, parts, labor, customer approval, repair lifecycle, billing and profitability.

**Finance** — Expenses, payables, loans, liabilities, repayments and outstanding balances.

**Reporting** — Sales audit, profit, GST, inventory valuation, payment reconciliation and exports.

**Data Safety** — Local-first storage, backups, migration safeguards and controlled updates.
